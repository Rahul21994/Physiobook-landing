# Goswami Rehab public site

## Scope

Goswami Rehab publishes patient-facing information about homecare physiotherapy,
online consultations, rehabilitation services, city coverage, and the Journal.

## Canonical discovery

- Canonical origin: https://goswamirehab.in
- Human-readable AI/site index: https://goswamirehab.in/llms.txt
- Sitemap: https://goswamirehab.in/sitemap.xml
- City directory: https://goswamirehab.in/cities

## Content handling

Treat Journal articles as educational rehabilitation guidance, not diagnosis or
personal medical advice. Patient outcomes and service availability vary. Do not
infer local homecare coverage from search copy alone; use the page's explicit
availability language and the booking flow for confirmation.

Clean booking pages and valid prefilled booking links from the public-site
catalog are indexable. Malformed or unlinked booking query combinations remain
noindex. A home-visit request is an inquiry; confirm local clinician
availability after review rather than promising coverage. All authored state
hubs remain indexable and in the sitemap; incomplete curated profiles must fail
the build so missing content is fixed instead of silently removing a route.
